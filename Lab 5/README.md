This lab project involved building a machine learning pipeline to predict customer Subscription Status by applying advanced feature engineering to a shopping behavior dataset. We began by cleaning the data and defining our target, then moved into creating high-impact features such as a Loyalty_Score (combining purchase history with satisfaction) and simplifying complex categories like item names to reduce noise. To ensure the model was both accurate and interpretable, we used a Random Forest Classifier wrapped in a professional pipeline that automated data transformation, allowing the AI to focus on key drivers like purchase frequency and spend amounts rather than irrelevant details like item color.
Through iterative testing, we discovered that the model's performance was highly sensitive to how we grouped data; specifically, we found that focusing on "high-frequency" shoppers and selecting only the top 25 most popular items created the best balance between detail and simplicity. By including a final Feature Selection step, we pruned the model of "weak" variables, which improved its ability to generalize to new customers. The final result was a lean, efficient model that not only achieved high accuracy but also provided clear insights into which customer behaviors, primarily shopping habits and loyalty, most strongly influence the decision to join a subscription program.
